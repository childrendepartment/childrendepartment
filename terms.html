Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At the Children's Ministry, protecting the privacy of our students, parents, and ministry volunteers is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect through our online services and how we handle, store, and protect it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal personal data necessary to manage Sunday School divisions, organize events, and maintain safe engagement:

  • Account & Demographic Info: Name, age, birthday, gender, division/class assignment, email address, and optional bio descriptions.
  • Profile Photos: Images uploaded voluntarily for profile identification within student directories.
  • Attendance Records: Sunday School attendance marks recorded by authorized teachers.
  • Technical Log Data: Basic usage data managed via Google Firebase services for authentication and database security.

2. How Information is Used

Information gathered is strictly utilized for educational and ministry purposes, including:

  • Organizing students into appropriate age divisions (Beginner, Primary, Middler).
  • Enabling teachers to maintain accurate weekly class attendance records.
  • Facilitating directory visibility exclusively between enrolled peers, teachers, and ministry leaders.
  • Notifying users regarding upcoming ministry events and schedule changes.
Notice Regarding Directory Visibility: Member names, class divisions, and profile pictures are visible to verified classmates and teachers to support community building within the ministry. Private email addresses and account passwords are strictly non-public.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is securely stored using Google Firebase Realtime Database and Authentication services. We enforce client-side sanitization and server-side rules to prevent unauthorized database access, code injections, or data leaks.

4. Account Control & Deletion

Users maintain complete ownership over their data. You can edit your personal details or permanently remove your account and stored database nodes at any time through your Account Settings page.

Terms of Service

Last Updated: September 2026

By accessing or using the Children's Ministry digital portal, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following Terms of Service. Please read them carefully.

1. Code of Conduct

Our platform is designed to provide a safe, respectful, and encouraging environment. All users agree to:

  • Provide accurate and truthful registration information.
  • Refrain from uploading inappropriate content, photos, or text.
  • Respect fellow classmates, teachers, and ministry leaders.
  • Maintain the confidentiality of their account login credentials.

2. Role-Based Access Controls

Access permissions are governed strictly by administrative roles:

  • Students/Members: Granted access to personal profile settings, event schedules, and division directories.
  • Teachers: Authorized to publish ministry events, manage student class lists, and track attendance.

3. Service Availability & Modifications

We strive to maintain high availability for our portal services but reserve the right to modify, suspend, or update platform functions or policies at any time to enhance system safety or ministry alignment.

Children's Digital Safety & COPPA Compliance

Recognizing that our platform serves children and young students, we strictly adhere to principles protecting minor safety on the internet:

  • We never sell, lease, or monetize user data to third-party advertisers or commercial entities.
  • Parental oversight is encouraged for all account creations involving minor students.
  • Any reported instance of inappropriate content or security concerns will result in immediate content removal and administrative account review.
